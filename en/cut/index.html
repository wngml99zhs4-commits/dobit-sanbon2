A design cut
tailored to your face and hair

Dobit Salon Sanbon 2 offers design cuts such as layered, hush and leaf cuts. Before cutting, a one-on-one hair consultation looks at your face shape, hair type and lifestyle to suggest a length and layering that bring out your own mood.

For men, texture cuts and see-through dandy cuts can be planned together with iron perms and down perms. We also offer healing spa cuts for men and women and 16-inch hair extensions. If you want waves too, see our hush and hippie perm guide.

Recommended for you if…

  • You want a style with visible layers, like a layered or hush cut
  • You want advice on the length and bangs that suit your face shape
  • You are looking for a men’s cut such as a texture cut or see-through dandy cut
  • Your side hair sticks out and you want a cut with a down perm

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which designer is best for a men’s cut?
Texture cuts, leaf cuts, see-through dandy cuts, iron perms and down perms are Jiwon’s specialties. Please choose your designer when booking.
Who specializes in layered cuts?
Layered cuts are a specialty of Seoji. Seoji holds Toni&Guy cutting diplomas.
How much is a junior cut?
A junior cut starts from ₩20,000 and a junior design cut from ₩23,000. Additional charges may apply depending on the designer’s level, hair length and products used.
