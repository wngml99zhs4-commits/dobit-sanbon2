Hair treatment
matched to your hair

Dobit Salon Sanbon 2 offers three levels of hair treatment: Special, Premium and Prestige. During a one-on-one hair consultation we look at your hair together and choose the right level.

If you want to keep your hair smooth before or after a perm, color or bleach, let us know during the consultation. See our perm and color menus.

Recommended for you if…

  • You perm or color often and want smoother hair
  • You need care for your hair after bleaching
  • You want to decide the right level through consultation

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What treatments do you offer and how much are they?
There are three: Special Treatment ₩70,000, Premium Treatment ₩100,000 and Prestige Treatment ₩150,000.
I don’t know which level I need.
Dobit Salon Sanbon 2 offers one-on-one hair consultations that look at your face shape, hair type and lifestyle. Tell us about your hair concerns when you visit and we will decide together.
Do I need to book a treatment?
You can book on Naver or by phone (031-396-1007). We are open Mon–Fri 11:00–20:00, Sat 10:00–20:00 and Sun 10:00–19:00.
