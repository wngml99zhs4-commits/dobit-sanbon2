From hush and hippie perms
to volume straightening

Dobit Salon Sanbon 2 offers women’s perms such as hush, hippie, build, vintage, wave and C-curl perms, men’s perms such as iron and down perms, and volume straightening, straightening + setting and repair perms.

After a one-on-one consultation on your face shape, hair type and lifestyle, we decide curl size and where the volume sits. Setting/digital perms, volume straightening, straightening + setting and repair perms are priced in S, M and L tiers. Planning a cut too? See our layered cut guide.

Recommended for you if…

  • You want natural waves such as a hush, hippie or vintage perm
  • You want to tidy frizzy hair with volume straightening
  • You are looking for a C-curl or build perm to go with a layered cut
  • You want a men’s perm such as an iron perm or down perm

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who specializes in hush and hippie perms?
Hush perms are a specialty of Seoji, Yua and Aki, and hippie perms of Seoji and Yua. Build, tassel and layered C-curl perms are Yoonseul’s specialties.
Do you do men’s perms?
Yes. Men’s basic perms start from ₩90,000 and down perms from ₩30,000. Iron perms and down perms are Jiwon’s specialties.
Can the perm price differ from the list?
Prices shown are base prices; additional charges may apply depending on the designer’s level, hair length and products used. For an exact price, please ask via Naver booking or by phone (031-396-1007).
