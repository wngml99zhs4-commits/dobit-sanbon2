From trend colors
to bleach and balayage

Dobit Salon Sanbon 2 offers trend colors such as ash beige, matte brown, red violet and brown, as well as design colors including bleach and balayage.

Designers trained in Vidal Sassoon and Toni&Guy cutting and Wella, L’Oréal and Milbon color work with you one-on-one to choose a color that suits your mood and image. Worried about your hair’s condition? See our hair treatment menu.

Recommended for you if…

  • You want a trendy color such as ash beige or matte brown
  • You want a bright tone or design color after bleaching
  • You want a natural gradation like balayage or ombré
  • You are looking for men’s color or special color

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much is bleaching?
Bleach A starts from ₩110,000 and Bleach B from ₩130,000. Additional charges may apply depending on the designer’s level, hair length and products used.
Who should I see for balayage or ombré?
Balayage is a specialty of vice director Aki. Yoonseul holds a Balayage & Ombré Diploma, and Yua and director Juhee have completed L’Oréal ombré & balayage technique training.
Do you do men’s hair color?
Yes. Men’s color is ₩90,000 and men’s special color starts from ₩130,000.
