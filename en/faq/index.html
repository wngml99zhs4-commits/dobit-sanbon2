FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

If you have any other questions, call us at 031-396-1007.

Home/FAQ
How do I book?
You can book via Naver booking or by phone (031-396-1007).
Is there parking?
Yes. You can use the building’s parking lot free for 3 hours.
What are your opening hours and holidays?
Mon–Fri 11:00–20:00, Sat 10:00–20:00 and Sun 10:00–19:00. We are open all year and closed only on the day of Seollal and Chuseok.
How do I find the salon?
From Exit 3 of Sanbon Station, walk straight toward the Central Fountain and E-mart → turn left at the end of Sanbon Rodeo Street → Yugong Plaza, opposite Shinhan Bank (the building with a side-dish shop on the 1st floor), 3rd floor.
Can prices differ from the list?
Prices shown are base prices; additional charges may apply depending on the designer’s level, hair length and products used.
It’s my first visit. How does it work?
We start with a one-on-one hair consultation on your face shape, hair type and lifestyle, and suggest a style that brings out your own mood.
Can I book a specific designer?
You can see the specialties of all 6 designers on our designers page. To book a specific designer, please use Naver booking or call us.
Do you do junior cuts?
Yes. A junior cut starts from ₩20,000 and a junior design cut from ₩23,000.
Do you do bleach and balayage?
Yes. Bleach A starts from ₩110,000 and Bleach B from ₩130,000. Bleach and balayage are vice director Aki’s specialties, and bleach is also director Juhee’s.
Do you do hair extensions?
Yes. Hair extensions (16 inch) start from ₩350,000.
