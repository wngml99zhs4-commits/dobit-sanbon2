About

Find the style that suits you
through one-on-one consultation

Dobit Salon Sanbon 2 is on the 3rd floor of Yugong Plaza, 78 Gwangjeong-ro, Gunpo-si — past Sanbon Rodeo Street from Exit 3 of Sanbon Station, opposite Shinhan Bank. Opened in 2021, our 100-pyeong salon has 15 styling chairs and 6 designers including the head director.

We offer design cuts such as layered, hush and leaf cuts; women’s perms such as hush, hippie, build, vintage, wave and C-curl perms; men’s hair including iron and down perms; trend colors such as ash beige and matte brown; bleach, balayage and hair extensions. A one-on-one consultation on your face shape, hair type and lifestyle helps us suggest a style that brings out your own mood.

What makes us different

  • 6 designers, including the head director, each focus on cuts, perms or color.
  • Designers trained in Vidal Sassoon and Toni&Guy cutting and Wella, L’Oréal and Milbon color handle design colors such as bleach, balayage and ombré.
  • One-on-one hair consultation that considers your face shape, hair type and lifestyle.
  • Healing spa cuts for men and women, and 16-inch hair extensions.
  • A 100-pyeong salon with 15 styling chairs and 3 hours of free parking in the building.
